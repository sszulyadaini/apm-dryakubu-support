Dr Yakubu Birni Da Karkara Support Group — Building Bridges Between Urban & Rural Communities

Under the umbrella of the Allied Peoples' Movement (APM), Dr Yakubu Birni Da Karkara champions inclusive growth that brings the opportunities of the city to rural areas while preserving the strengths of Karkara.

Birni (City) Vision

Modern infrastructure, youth employment, urban services, and transparent local governance to make our towns hubs of opportunity.

Karkara (Rural) Vision

Sustainable agriculture, clean water, rural health initiatives, and market access to strengthen village livelihoods.

About Us

The Dr Yakubu Birni Da Karkara Support Group is a grassroots movement aligned with the Allied Peoples' Movement (APM). We bring together citizens from both urban and rural communities to co-design projects that improve livelihoods, expand services, and foster accountable leadership.

Mission

Promote inclusive development linking Birni and Karkara through infrastructure, education, and economic programs.

Core Values

Service, transparency, community-led solutions, and sustainable progress.

Leadership

Led by Dr Yakubu, our leadership focuses on pragmatic policies that balance urban modernization with rural resilience under APM's platform.
